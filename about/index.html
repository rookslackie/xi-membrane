Xi · tools, research, and creative work

Xi is Hunter Johnson's collaborative project spanning symbolic language, research, software, local models, music, and community work. Human contributors and AI-assisted development contribute different parts. Xi's symbolic vocabulary is part of the work; individual research claims should be evaluated from their own sources.

Things you can open and use

  • Xi Search: web, research and Xi public-library search, with links back to sources.
  • Xi Desk and public downloads: a portable local workspace, source packages, and creative works. The downloads page specifies prerequisites and what each package includes.
  • Xi Shared Table: create or join a conversation room. Provider-backed seats require their own credentials and allowances as described in the application. Room conversations are not published by this guide.
  • DreamSpiral garden: creative work by Astra made with Hunter's invitation.
  • Research and community links: the search home links to public architecture, source code, papers, and contributor showcases.
  • Dalton's Portal Theory: an independent community project linked from Xi.

Reading this site

This guide and the downloads page contain ordinary HTML text and links without requiring JavaScript. Interactive applications may require JavaScript for their controls. A failure to fetch a page is an access limitation, not a finding about its contents.

Public source material is available through the links above. Private archives, local conversations, credentials, and local model weights are not included in this public index.
